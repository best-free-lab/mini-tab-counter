\# Privacy Policy — Mini Tab Counter



Last updated: November 2026



Mini Tab Counter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.



\- It only counts open tabs and windows when you open the popup.

\- It does not read page URLs, titles, or content.

\- It makes no network requests and uses no analytics or tracking.

\- It stores nothing, locally or remotely.

\- It requests no special browser permissions.
